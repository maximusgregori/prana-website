# Ashtanga Full Primary 90min

Ashtanga Full Primary (90 minutes) Full Primary follows the complete traditional Ashtanga Primary Series from start to finish — Sun Salutations, full standing sequence, full seated sequence, complete finishing postures, and final rest.
