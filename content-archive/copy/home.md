## Our Story

![Yoga practitioner in a deep backbend with dramatic warm lighting](https://www.pranawellness.love/_next/static/media/story-bg.fde8c79a.jpg)

Prana was created from a simple but deeply felt need:

a place to land.

What started with a desire to share holistic, fresh-ingredient smoothies became a gathering point for the east side of Austin. We added an outdoor cold plunge, hosted breathwork classes, and expanded our vision to the lot next door.

Not a place to rush through or perform wellness, but a place to return to — again and again — where movement feels intentional and community feels genuine.

"We are here to support presence, not perfection. To offer a space that holds you — whether you come to move, to breathe, or simply to be."

This is Prana. A place to return to.
